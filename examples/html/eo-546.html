Executive Orders

Executive Order No. 546 — Creating a Ministry of Public Works and a Ministry of Transportation and Communications

Status
Amended (superseding transfers under later EOs)
Effective Date
July 23, 1979
Issued By
President Ferdinand E. Marcos

This Order restructured government functions by separating public works from transportation and communications, creating two ministries pursuant to the President's continuing reorganization authority under Presidential Decree No. 1416.

Part A — Ministry of Public Works

Sections 1–5 create the Ministry of Public Works, defining its authority, functions (ports, flood control, buildings, water supply), organizational structure, and the operating relationship between the ministry proper, its bureaus, and regional offices.

Part B — Ministry of Transportation and Communications

Section 6. Creates the Ministry of Transportation and Communications as the primary policy and regulatory body for transportation and communications networks nationwide.

Sections 7–13. Establish the Ministry's authority, functions, and constituent bureaus: Bureau of Land Transportation, Bureau of Air Transportation, Bureau of Posts, and Bureau of Telecommunications.

Section 14 — National Telecommunications Commission

The Board of Communications and the Telecommunications Control Bureau are integrated into a single entity: the National Telecommunications Commission ("the Commission"). This is the founding provision establishing NTC as it operates today, later empowered further by RA 7925.

Section 15 — Functions of the Commission

The Commission is empowered to: issue Certificates of Public Convenience for communications utilities and services; regulate areas of operation and rates; grant radio frequency permits, including for amateur radio and broadcasting; sub-allocate ITU-assigned frequency bands; establish and enforce rules and standards; coordinate with other government agencies; promulgate rules to encourage effective and competitive use of communications facilities; supervise and inspect radio stations; license radio operators; and register radio transmitters and transceivers.

Section 16 — Organization of the Commission

The Commission is composed of a Commissioner and two Deputy Commissioners (preferably one lawyer, one economist), under the supervision and control of the Ministry except with respect to its quasi-judicial functions, which are independently appealable.

Part C — Other Provisions

Section 19. Abolished/Transferred Agencies. The Board of Communications and the Telecommunications Control Bureau are abolished, with functions transferred to NTC.

Section 23. Repealing Clause. All conflicting laws, decrees, charters, executive orders, administrative orders, proclamations, rules, and regulations are repealed or modified accordingly.

Section 24. Effectivity. This Executive Order took effect immediately upon issuance.

Done in the City of Manila, July 23, 1979.

Issued to keep pace with wireless technology developments, ensure wider access to the limited radio spectrum, and enable use of cost-effective technology, by adding Ultra-Wide Band (UWB) parameters to the Short Range Device (SRD) rules under MC 03-05-2007.

Section 1.16 — Definition of Ultra-Wide Band (UWB)

Per ITU-R Recommendation SM.1755, UWB is a short-range radiocommunication technology involving intentional generation/transmission of radio-frequency energy spread over a very large frequency range, potentially overlapping several allocated frequency bands. UWB devices typically radiate with either a −10 dB bandwidth of at least 500 MHz or a −10 dB fractional bandwidth greater than 0.2.

Section 2 — Operating Parameters/Conditions

Technical parameter limits must not be exceeded by any function or means of the equipment. Authorized band: 6.0–8.5 GHz, with a maximum mean EIRP of −41.3 dBm/MHz.

Operating conditions for UWB devices: (a) transmit only when sending information to an associated receiver, ceasing transmission within 10 seconds absent acknowledgement, and requiring re-acknowledgement at least every 10 seconds; (b) must be marked with supplier/manufacturer name and model/type reference, legibly and indelibly; (c) no external/accessible control permitting operation outside specification; (d) fixed integral antenna, no antenna connector; (e) may include interference-mitigating techniques for additional protection; (f) operate on an unprotected, non-interference basis — no protection claims against interference, and must not cause harmful interference to other allocated radiocommunication services; (g) must be tested for Safety, EMC, and RF efficiency — only NTC Type-Accepted UWB devices are permitted; (h) UWB device operation aboard aircraft, ship, or satellite is not permitted.

Effectivity

Any inconsistent circular, memorandum, order, or guideline (or part thereof) is superseded or amended. Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center. Quezon City, August 29, 2018.

Memorandum Circular No. 06-04-99 — Qualifications and Number of Radio Personnel Required On-Board Philippine Registered GMDSS Vessels

Status
Active
Issued
April 12, 1999, Quezon City
Issued By
Commissioner Joseph A. Santiago; Deputy Commissioners Nestor C. Dacanay and Aurelio M. Umali

Background

Issued in view of mandatory full compliance with the Global Maritime Distress and Safety System (GMDSS), effective February 1, 1999, as required by the Radio Regulations.

Minimum Personnel Requirements

Standard requirement: Two deck officers (not the master) holding a General Operator Certificate (GOC), or one exclusive radio officer holding both a General Operator Certificate (GOC) and a Radio Electronic Certificate (REC).

For ships with at-sea (on-board) maintenance capability: Two deck officers holding GOC (not the master) plus one non-exclusive radio officer holding both GOC and REC.

Effectivity

This Circular took effect immediately upon issuance, April 12, 1999.

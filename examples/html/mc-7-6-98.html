Memorandum Circular No. 7-6-98 — Amendments to Memorandum Circulars 2-1-97 and 1-1-98 and Providing Additional Licensing Guidelines and Procedures for Short Range Radio Service (SRRS)

Status
Amended (see MC 02-01-2014 addendum)
Adopted
June 25, 1998
Legal Basis
RA 7925; Act 3846, as amended; EO 546
Amends
MC 2-1-97 and MC 1-1-98
Issued By
Acting Commissioner Fidelo Q. Dumlao; Deputy Commissioners Consuelo S. Perez, Teodoro Y. Yabes

Section A — Definition of Terms

Short Range Radio Service (SRRS): a private radio communication system in the Land Mobile Service limited to portable transceivers for short-range communications. SRRS Special Permit: authority issued by NTC for possession/operation of an SRRS station. Type Acceptance: certification requiring no laboratory testing, based on an accepted foreign administration's prior type approval. Type Approval: certification requiring NTC laboratory testing. Also defines Portable unit and Repeater/relay system.

Section B — Technical Parameters

Frequencies are shared-use, non-interference basis. Linear amplifiers/boosters and external antennas strictly prohibited. Equipment must have fixed non-extendable frequency, max 2.5W portable output, FM voice emission, and be duly type approved/accepted. Frequency band: 325.0000–325.4875 MHz, 12.5 kHz channeling (40 channels). Data communication and PSTN interconnection are prohibited; external repeaters not allowed.

Section C — Application for Type Approval/Acceptance

Type Acceptance requires the acceptance fee, certified foreign type-approval certificate and test results, full technical documentation, and equipment photographs. Type Approval requires the approval fee, a model/prototype for testing, full technical documentation, and photographs. Equipment must carry a tamper-proof label per Annex B.

Section D — Importation of Radio Equipment

Import permits and special permit numbers issued only to NTC-authorized dealers upon fee payment; same fee applies to locally manufactured units. Dealers/manufacturers submit regular sales and stock reports by serial and permit number. Manufacturers (foreign or local) must be NTC-accredited; import authority denied if the source isn't an NTC-accredited manufacturer.

Section E — Licensing, Collection, and Fees

One-time Special Permit fee: ₱1,000/unit, collected from authorized dealers upon sale. Type Approval fees: Filing ₱150, Laboratory ₱5,000, Certificate ₱1,200. Type Acceptance fees: Filing ₱150, Certificate ₱1,200. Permit issuance centralized at NTC's Radio Regulations and Licensing Division (RRLD) unless modified.

Sections F–G — Administrative Sanction, Effectivity

Unpermitted possession/operation, sale by non-accredited dealers, or failure to report sales/stock is handled under existing Radio Laws and Regulations. Effective 15 days after publication; supersedes inconsistent prior issuances. Adopted June 25, 1998, Quezon City.

Attachment A — Technical Standards and Specifications

Detailed equipment specification: max 3W transmitter output at 12.5 kHz channel spacing, operating on 325.0000–325.4875 MHz across 40 channels (listed in Annex A). Requires CTCSS sub-audible squelch capability (10+ tones from a standard list), unique antenna fittings, and full transmitter/receiver test procedures covering carrier power, modulation limiting, conducted spurious emissions, and frequency error (±3ppm tolerance) under both standard and extreme temperature/voltage test conditions.

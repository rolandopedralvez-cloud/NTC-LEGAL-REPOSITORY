Memorandum Circular No. 07-08-85 — Guidelines and Procedures for Issuance of Restricted Radiotelephone Operator's Certificate for Land Mobile Station (RLM), Without Examination, to Qualified Applicants

Status
Active
Issued
August 1985
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended
Issued By
Commissioner Ceferino S. Carreon

Sections 1–2 — Definition and Scope of Authority

An RLM Certificate authorizes radiotelephone service of a duly licensed land mobile/portable station. Holders may operate only external controls (on/off, volume, channel, squelch), must follow license conditions, and follow base operator instructions.

Section 3 — Applicant Qualifications

Philippine citizen, good moral character, 18+ years old, able to transmit/receive in Pilipino or English, employed by a firm authorized to operate land mobile stations, and completed an accredited Radio Training School (RTS) course on rules, regulations, and operating procedures.

Sections 4–5 — Study Course, RTS Accreditation

Study course (4 hours total): laws/regulations (2 hrs), traffic handling/interference prevention (1 hr), equipment operation (1 hr). RTS accreditation requires qualified instructors and adequate facilities; the Commission may also conduct direct briefings for government personnel. See later expansion in MC 10-12-95.

Sections 6–9 — Application, Issuance, Renewal, Duplicates

Requires completion certificate, employer certification, and copy of the relevant station license/permit. Issued without examination upon compliance. Renewal must be filed 30 days before expiry; late renewal (up to 6 months) incurs 50% surcharge, 6 months–1 year incurs 100% surcharge (with a notice-based exception). Lost/destroyed certificates require a sworn statement for duplicate issuance.

Sections 10–14 — Validity, Fees, Sanctions, Effectivity

Maximum validity: 5 years. Fees: Application ₱20; Annual issuance ₱20/year; Duplicate ₱50. Suspension/revocation follows standard operator certificate procedures for violations. Supersedes inconsistent prior issuances. Effective immediately upon approval.

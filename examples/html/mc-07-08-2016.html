Memorandum Circular No. 07-08-2016 — Fixed Wireless Systems Operating in the 71-76 GHz and 81-86 GHz Bands

Status
Active
Issued
August 5, 2016
Legal Basis
Act 3846 (Radio Control Law); EO 546; RA 7925 and its IRR
Issued By
Deputy Commissioners Edgardo V. Cabarios, Delia M. Cordoba

Rationale

Issued per RA 7925's IRR requiring periodic spectrum review, and pursuant to ITU Radiocommunication Sector Recommendation ITU-R F.2006 (03/2012), which sets frequency channel/block arrangements for Fixed Wireless Systems (FWS) in these bands. Addresses growing demand for high-capacity broadband transport, including uncompressed HDTV signal transmission and mobile network applications.

Sections 1–3 — Allocation, Channelling Plan, Type Approval

Allocates 71–76 GHz and 81–86 GHz to FWS as part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT). Channelling plan based on ITU-R F.2006, provided in Annex 1 (channel sizes from 250 MHz up to 2.5 GHz block arrangements). Only type-approved/type-accepted radio equipment may operate in these bands.

Sections 4–5 — Licensing, Fees, and Spectrum User Fees (SUF)

Equipment undergoes standard Point-to-Point Radio System licensing with corresponding administrative fees. Annual SUF: for paired (FDD) systems, ₱20,000 per 250 MHz of bandwidth per station; for unpaired systems, ₱15,000 per 250 MHz of bandwidth per station.

Sections 6–7 — Repealing Clause, Effectivity

Inconsistent prior issuances are amended/superseded. Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center. August 5, 2016, Quezon City.

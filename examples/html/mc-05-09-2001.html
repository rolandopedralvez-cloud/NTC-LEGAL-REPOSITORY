Memorandum Circular No. 05-09-2001 — Guidelines for the Issuance of a Certificate of Competency and Endorsement (COC-E) to a Holder of Radio Electronic Certificate (REC) in Accordance with Regulation I/2, Paragraph 4 of the STCW Convention

Status
Active
Issued
September 11, 2001
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended; EO 546; ITU Radio Regulations Ch. NIX Art. 55; STCW-95 Convention Reg. I/2 and Article VI
Issued By
Commissioner Eliseo M. Rio Jr.; Deputy Commissioners Kathleen G. Heceta, Armi Jane R. Borje

Eligibility Requirements

A Certificate of Competency and Endorsement (COC-E) under the STCW 1978 Convention (as amended 1995) is issued to a qualified individual who: (a) holds a valid Radio Electronic Certificate (REC) or has passed the required REC examination; and (b) is presently serving on an international sea-going vessel as radio electronic officer/technician, or has employer certification of intended hire in that role.

Certificate Details and Fees

The COC-E bears the same control number as the holder's REC and shares its validity period, not to exceed 5 years. Annual Certificate and Endorsement fee: ₱380.00. Issued in the standard security form prescribed by the Maritime Training Council, signed by the Commissioner.

Compliance and Effectivity

Applicants must strictly adhere to these guidelines; misrepresentation is grounds for revocation/suspension and applicable penalties. Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior issuances. September 11, 2001, Quezon City.

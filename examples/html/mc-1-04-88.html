Memorandum Circular No. 1-04-88 — Rules and Regulations Governing Equipment Provided by Customers/Subscribers of Public Networks

Status
Amended (see MC 02-01-2001 for revised interface standards)
Issued
April 1988
Legal Basis
Public Service Act, as amended; DOTC Circular 87-188
Issued By
Commissioner Jose Luis A. Alcuaz; Approved by Secretary Rainerio O. Reyes (DOTC)

Section 1 — Purpose

Defines conditions/procedures for CPE type approval; rules for CPE supplier accreditation; supplier responsibilities; conditions for connecting customer-supplied CPE to public networks; and network operator responsibilities regarding connected CPE.

Section 2 — Type Approval

Type approval ensures a CPE will not cause network harm (irreversible equipment damage, electrical hazards, billing malfunction, signal noise) or operational defects (incorrect signaling, false answers, call failures). Required for new CPE types, modifications, and trade name/model changes. Pre-existing legally connected CPE is grandfathered. An Interim Type Approval Board (PETEF, ITESAP, NTC, and user groups) formulates interface standards.

Section 3 — Accreditation of CPE Suppliers

Requires ₱250,000 paid-up capitalization, one full-time licensed ECE engineer (1+ year experience or formal training), two full-time qualified technicians, 2-year spare parts stock, valid distributorship agreement, and proof of type-approved products. Accreditation Certificates are valid 4 years, renewable. Grounds for suspension/revocation include selling non-type-approved CPE or unauthorized network connection.

Sections 4–6 — Responsibilities

CPE suppliers must secure type approval, maintain repair capability, ensure production consistency, and report to the Commission. Network operators must allow non-discriminatory connection of type-approved CPE, provide 1-year advance notice of network interface changes, avoid unreasonable rates, and may disconnect CPE causing network harm or fraud (with 15-day prior written notice except where impractical).

Section 7 — Responsibility of the Customer/Subscriber

Subscribers must apply properly before connecting CPE, maintain it safely without tampering, secure approval for modifications, and cooperate in maintaining network integrity.

Sections 8–11 — Tariffs, Violations, Repeal, Effectivity

Network operators must file necessary tariff revisions within 6 months. Violations are subject to administrative sanctions. Inconsistent prior issuances are superseded. Effective immediately upon issuance; includes a full authorized CPE equipment list (PBXs, Key Telephone Systems, modems, facsimile equipment, teleprinters, paging equipment, and more) as Table A.

Memorandum Circular No. 10-12-95 — Additional Guidelines in the Conduct of Restricted Land Mobile (RLM) Seminar

Status
Active
Issued
December 29, 1995
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended
Issued By
Commissioner Simeon L. Kintanar

I. Guidelines and Procedures

Applicants must file a letter-request at least 2 weeks before the proposed seminar date, listing the venue and at least 25 prospective participants. The venue must have a microphone, overhead projector, and/or writing board. Lecturers are assigned by Regional Directors or the Radio Regulations and Licensing Division (RRLD) from a certified pool of qualified NTC lecturers; Regional Directors submit certified lecturer lists to the Commissioner through RRLD.

II. Qualifications of the Lecturer

Must be technically knowledgeable in radio station operations and relevant laws; at least an Engineer II with one year of Commission employment; must have attended an RRLD seminar on proper RLM lecturing; must have experience processing/evaluating radio station applications; and must be proficient in radio station message handling.

III. Scope of the Lecture

Covers: (a) NTC's mandate and organizational structure with emphasis on licensing units; (b) radio regulations and licensing procedures across radio services, including inspection, apprehension, and confiscation procedures; (c) subject matter defined in MC 7-08-85, with emphasis on Sections 12 and 13 of Act No. 3846, as amended.

Effectivity

Supersedes or amends inconsistent existing circulars, orders, or instructions. Took effect immediately upon approval, December 29, 1995, Quezon City.

Memorandum Circular No. 13-09-2004 — Modified Administrative Fee for Value Added Services

Status
Active
Issued
September 24, 2004
Amends
MC 19-12-2000 (Revised Schedule of Administrative Fees and Charges)

Fee Schedule

Certificate of Registration (up to 5 Value Added Services): ₱6,000 annual. Additional service beyond 5: ₱1,000/service/year. Registrations with fewer than 5 services are charged the same flat ₱6,000 outright fee. Registrants may modify a certificate to add services up to or beyond five by paying the applicable modification and filing fees. All other existing fees and charges not revised herein remain in effect.

Effectivity

Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies furnished to the UP Law Center. Issued September 24, 2004, Quezon City.

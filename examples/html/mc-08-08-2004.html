Memorandum Circular No. 08-08-2004 — Additional Rules and Regulations on the Purchase, Sale, Lease and/or Retail of Mobile Phones

Status
Amended (see MC 08-08-2004A)
Issued
August 6, 2004
Legal Basis

Forms Part Of
MC 1-04-88 and MC 02-01-2001
Issued By
Commissioner Ronald Olivar Solis; Deputy Commissioners Kathleen G. Heceta, Jorge V. Sarmiento

Sections 1–2 — Purpose and Definitions

Enables NTC to monitor and regulate purchase, sale, lease, and retail of mobile phone units, parts, and accessories. Defines Mobile Phone Dealer (MPD) and Mobile Phone Dealer's Permit (MPDP).

Section 3 — General Provisions

MPDs require a valid permit; may not stock illegally acquired or non-type-approved phones. Must register all stock by IMEI code and SIM numbers monthly. Existing CPE/radio dealers had 30 days from effectivity to notify NTC if expanding into mobile phones; unpermitted existing establishments had 30 days to secure permits or face closure/fines.

Section 4 — Operating Guidelines

MPDs must comply with this Circular and existing radio laws; may not deal in phones from unauthorized/illegal sources or advertise such; must post a signboard (min. 50cm x 100cm) with business name, services, address, and permit number; must submit monthly sales/stock reports.

Section 5 — Permits and Applications

Requires SEC/DTI registration and business permit, valid dealership agreement, 6-month spare parts stock, and ₱100,000 minimum paid-up capital. Fees: Filing ₱180; Permit ₱1,200/year; Inspection ₱720/year. Non-payment grounds for permit denial/suspension/revocation.

Sections 6–9 — Validity, Inspection, Sanctions, Revocation Procedure

Permits valid 1 year, renewable. Pre-approval inspections with a 30-day cure period for deficiencies. Fines of ₱5,000 per unit/violation for illegal sourcing, missing type-approval labels, illegal importation, or operating without a permit (plus closure). Revocation follows a show-cause process with a 15-day response window; Cease and Desist Orders may issue pending hearing.

Sections 10–11 — Repealing Clause, Effectivity

Inconsistent prior issuances are amended or superseded. Effective 15 days after publication, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center. August 6, 2004, Quezon City.

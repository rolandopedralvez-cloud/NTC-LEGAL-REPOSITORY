Memorandum Circular No. 11-12-2001 — Amendments to Section 4 (Spectrum User Fee Rates for Cellular Mobile Telephone Service and Spread Spectrum Radio Stations) and Section 5 of MC 10-10-97

Status
Active
Issued
December 13, 2001
Amends
MC 10-10-97 (Spectrum User Fees), Sections 4 and 5
Issued By
Commissioner Eliseo M. Rio, Jr.; Deputy Commissioners Kathleen G. Heceta, Armi Jane R. Borje

Section 4.1 — Cellular Mobile Telephone Service SUF

SUF is based on the total bandwidth (TX + RX combined) assigned per CMTS operator: ₱500,000/MHz for 20 MHz and below; ₱1,000,000/MHz for the portion in excess of 20 MHz but not exceeding 30 MHz; ₱2,000,000/MHz for the portion in excess of 30 MHz but not exceeding 40 MHz; ₱5,000,000/MHz for any portion in excess of 40 MHz.

Section 4.12 — Spread Spectrum Radio Stations

For Spread Spectrum Radio Stations (Point-to-Point or Point-to-Multipoint), SUF is based on the occupied (transmitted) bandwidth at a given instant, per the rates prescribed in Sections 4.7 and 4.8 of MC 10-10-97.

Section 5 — Permit to Purchase and New Station Provisions

No SUF is required for a first-time Permit to Purchase (valid up to 6 months, renewable for meritorious reasons upon SUF payment; if renewed after June 30, 50% of the annual SUF applies). New radio stations licensed after June 30 pay 50% of the annual SUF for that year, full amount in succeeding years. These provisions (5.1–5.2) do not apply to stations operating within CMTS-allocated frequency bands.

Effectivity

Inconsistent prior issuances are amended, revised, or repealed. Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center. December 13, 2001, Quezon City.

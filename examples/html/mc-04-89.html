Memorandum Circular No. 04-89 — Revised Schedule of Administrative Sanctions for Violations of Radio Laws, Rules and Regulations

Status
Active
Issued
April 5, 1989
Effective
15 days after publication in the Official Gazette
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended; EO 546; other existing laws, rules and regulations
Issued By
Commissioner Jose Luis A. Alcuaz

Prescribes administrative sanctions under NTC's power to supervise and inspect radio stations and telecommunications facilities, as established under EO 546 and reinforced by RA 7925.

I. Illegal Possession, Installation/Construction, and/or Operation

Sets fines for individuals and companies covering: illegal purchase/possession of radio transmitters/transceivers (with equipment forfeiture); illegal installation/construction with or without permit; allowing operation by an unlicensed operator; and use of unauthorized frequencies. Individual fines range from ₱1,000–₱2,000; company/corporation fines range from ₱2,500–₱5,000, several carrying equipment forfeiture.

II. Violation of Terms and Conditions of a Radio Construction or License

Covers operating beyond authorized hours, allowing operation by an operator with inappropriate license, excessive calling (maritime stations), unauthorized equipment, unauthorized power, and unauthorized emission. Fines range ₱1,000–₱3,500 depending on violation and entity type.

III. Operating a Radio Training School Without a Certificate of Recognition

Fine of ₱2,000 for other violations relative to radio training school operations.

IV. Failure to Install Appropriate Radio Facilities as Required

Fine of ₱3,000.

V. Illegal Sale

₱2,000 per unit for sale by an authorized dealer to a non-authorized person/entity, and for sale by an unauthorized dealer (with equipment forfeiture in the latter case).

VI. For Other Violations

₱2,000 per count for violations not otherwise specified. A ₱500 fine applies for failure to maintain a station logbook.

Commissioner's Discretion

The Commissioner(s) may increase or decrease the schedule of fines, or declare confiscation and forfeiture of equipment, or suspend or revoke any permit or license in the interest of public welfare, safety, or security, or as warranted by the facts and circumstances.

Effectivity

This Circular may be revised, revoked, or amended as the need arises, and takes effect 15 days following publication in the Official Gazette. Issued in Quezon City, April 5, 1989.

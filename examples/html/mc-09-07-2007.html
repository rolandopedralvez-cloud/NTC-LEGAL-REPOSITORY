Memorandum Circular No. 09-07-2007 — Rules on the Interconnection of Local Exchange Carriers in Local Calling Areas

Status
Active
Issued
July 19, 2007
Legal Basis
EO 546, EO 59, RA 7925
Issued By
Commissioner Abraham R. Abesamis; Deputy Commissioners Jorge V. Sarmiento, Jaime M. Fortes Jr.

Background

Issued after multiple local exchange carriers (LECs) operating in the same local calling area sought Commission intervention for network interconnection. Local exchange networks are capital-intensive with economies of scale, and it's an LEC's responsibility to carry subscriber traffic to interconnecting LECs' networks.

Rules 1–3 — No Charges, Trunk Requirements, Radio Facilities

No interconnection or access charges apply between interconnected LECs within a local calling area. Each LEC must provide sufficient trunks/circuits to guarantee P01 grade of service, with termination at the nearest exchange (or same municipality/city exchange for multi-exchange LECs). Where radio facilities are needed, LECs must provide space/termination free of charge and allow 24-hour-notice access for repair/maintenance.

Rules 4–6 — Timelines and Negotiation

Interconnection must be effected within 90 days of notice by one LEC (copying the Commission). Existing non-interconnected LECs must interconnect within 180 days of this Circular's effectivity. LECs may negotiate better terms, but if no agreement is reached within 90 days, the default rules (1–3) apply.

Rules 7–8 — Repealing Clause, Effectivity

Amends or supersedes inconsistent prior issuances. Effective 15 days after publication, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center. July 19, 2007, Quezon City.

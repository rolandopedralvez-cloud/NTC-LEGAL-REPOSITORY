Memorandum Circular No. 17-2-2002 — Rules and Regulations Governing Radio Training Centers (RTC) Offering Short-Term GMDSS Radio Communications Courses

Status
Active
Issued
December 20, 2002
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended; EO 546; ITU Radio Regulations Ch. NIX Art. 55; STCW-78 Convention Regs I/6, I/8
Issued By
Commissioner Armi Jane R. Borje; Deputy Commissioners Kathleen G. Heceta, Jorge V. Sarmiento

Sections I–IV — Scope, Definitions, General Provisions, Accreditation Requirements

Applies to all Radio Training Centers (RTCs) offering short-term GMDSS courses (SRROC, INMARSAT, GMDSS, REOC). No entity may offer such courses without a Certificate of Accreditation from NTC; certificates from non-accredited RTCs are not honored. Accreditation requires extensive administrative documentation (Letter of Intent, SEC/DTI registration, feasibility study, curriculum, instructor list, equipment inventory) plus technical permits (Purchase, Possess, Construction, Radio Station License).

Sections V–X — Courses, Entry Standards, Curriculum, Facilities, Teaching Materials

Offers GOC courses (SRROC, INMARSAT, GMDSS — 136 total hours) and REC course (REOC — 144 hours). Entry requires minimum age 21 and licensing prerequisites depending on course. Class caps: 24 students lecture, 5–6 per practical group. Facility and equipment specifications are extensive, including operational MF/HF/VHF transceivers, EPIRB, SART, NAVTEX receiver, INMARSAT-C terminal, and (for REOC) radar, gyrocompass, GPS, and full electronics test equipment.

Sections XI–XIV — Certification, Faculty, Instruction Delivery

RTCs issue completion certificates. Instructors must complete IMO Model Course 6.09 training plus role-specific qualifications (3+ years GMDSS vessel experience, relevant licenses/degrees, teaching experience). Instruction must follow prescribed syllabi and IMO Model Course guidance. Weekly graduate reports required.

Sections XV–XIX — Accreditation, Radio Station License, Inspection, Renewal

Certificates and Radio Station Licenses issued for up to 3 years. NTC conducts pre-approval inspections; non-conformances get a 30-day cure period. Renewal filings due 3 months before expiration, with surcharges of 50%–100% for late filing (up to 1 year); non-filing voids recognition of subsequent training.

Section XVIII — Assigned Frequencies

Specifies DSC, radiotelephony, radiotelex, VHF Channels 16/70, and NAVTEX frequency bands reserved for radio training station use.

Sections XX–XXI — Grounds for Denial/Revocation, Fee Schedule

Accreditation may be denied/revoked for non-compliance with requirements, radio laws, standards, or fee payment. Fees: Filing ₱200; Permit to Purchase ₱96 (VHF/UHF) / ₱120 (HF/SSB); Permit to Possess ₱60 / ₱96; Construction Permit ₱500; Inspection ₱500/year; Radio Station License ₱1,500/year; Certificate of Accreditation ₱5,000/year.

Effectivity

Effective immediately; may be revised/revoked/amended by law. Formal effectivity 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies filed with the UP Law Center. December 20, 2002, Quezon City.

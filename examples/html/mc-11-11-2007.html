Memorandum Circular No. 11-11-2007 — Guidelines for the Operation of Digital FM Radio Broadcast

Status
Active
Adopted
November 26, 2007
Legal Basis
Act 3846; EO 546; RA 8792 (Electronic Commerce Act)
Issued By
Commissioner Ruel V. Canobas; Deputy Commissioners Jorge V. Sarmiento, Jaime M. Fortes, Jr.

Section 1 — Definition of Terms

Digital FM Radio Broadcast: technology enabling FM stations to broadcast programs digitally as discrete samples of a sound waveform. Also defines Digital FM Radio Broadcast station, Enhanced FM Radio Broadcast service (value-added features beyond regular FM), FM Radio Broadcast station, Analog FM signal, and Digital FM signal.

Section 2 — General Provisions

Authorized analog FM stations may operate digital FM in parallel, without disrupting/terminating analog service absent Commission approval. Digital signal transmits on the same frequency as the existing analog station. Program content must match the analog signal, except multicast programming requires prior Commission authorization. Enhanced/value-added services permitted subject to existing rules. Digital equipment must be co-located at the analog station's transmitter site. Digital signal coverage limited to the licensed analog service area, never exceeding the 60 dBu contour, and must remain 20 dB below the analog signal level regardless of transmission method (high/low combining or separate antenna). New applicants seeking to operate digital FM directly undergo the standard authorization process. The 800 kHz minimum frequency separation for same-service-area FM stations is maintained.

Section 3 — Authorization / Permits

Existing analog operators must file a Motion under their original case number showing financial/technical capability for digital service. Permits to Purchase/Possess and a Construction Permit are required before equipment acquisition/installation. A Propagation Permit is required before actual digital operation. Operators offering enhanced services must register with the Commission.

Sections 4–5 — Repealing Clause, Effectivity

Inconsistent prior issuances are amended/suspended accordingly. Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center.

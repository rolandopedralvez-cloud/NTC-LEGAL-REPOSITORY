Memorandum Circular No. 16-11-2004 — Rules and Regulations Governing Radio Training Schools (RTS) Offering Communications Technician Course (CTC)

Status
Active
Issued
November 9, 2004
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended; EO 546; Department Orders 7 and 23
Issued By
Commissioner Ronald Olivar Solis; Deputy Commissioners Jorge V. Sarmiento, Kathleen G. Heceta

Sections I–II — General Provisions, Accreditation Requirements

Requires NTC Certificate of Accreditation to offer the 2-year Communications Technician Course (CTC); non-accredited diplomas are not honored. Accreditation requires extensive admin documentation (Letter of Intent, SEC/DTI registration, feasibility study, curriculum, instructor list) plus technical requirements (lab equipment, permits, station license). Compare similar structure in MC 17-2-2002 for GMDSS courses.

Sections III–IV — Course and Subjects

2-year course; graduates qualify for the First Class Radiotelephone Operator's Examination. Curriculum covers electricity principles, radiotelephone/broadcast equipment operation and repair, direction-finding, telecom installation/calibration, radio law, antenna fundamentals, computer basics, transmission media, electronics/semiconductor principles, plus 240 hours of on-the-job training.

Sections V–VII — Entry Standards, Class Limits, Facilities

Entry requires high school graduation and good moral character. Class caps: 35 lecture, 6 practical group. Classrooms must be 42+ sq meters with detailed equipment requirements including oscilloscopes, signal generators, spectrum analyzers, and various trainer kits.

Sections VIII–XII — Teaching Aids, Certification, Faculty, Reporting

Faculty qualifications include electrical/electronics engineering degrees, BS Education with specialized training, licensed radiotelephone/telegraph operators with 5+ years' service, or government technical inspectors with 3+ years' experience. Semester enrollment/completion reports required.

Sections XIII–XVIII — Accreditation, License, Inspection, Frequencies, Renewal, Denial Grounds

Certificates/licenses valid up to 3 years. Assigned training frequencies include 2639/5220 kHz, 2835 kHz, 7520 kHz, and 149.19/149.25 MHz. Renewal due 3 months before expiry; late filing surcharges 50%–100%. Denial/revocation grounds include non-compliance with requirements, standards, or fee payment.

Sections XIX–XX — Fines and Fees

Fines range ₱2,500–₱5,000 per violation for illegal purchase/possession, installation, operation, unlicensed operator use, or unauthorized frequency use (several with equipment forfeiture). Fees: Filing ₱200; Permits ₱60–120; Construction ₱500; Inspection ₱500/yr; Radio Station License ₱1,000/yr; Accreditation ₱3,000/yr.

Sections XXI–XXII — Repealing Clause, Effectivity

Inconsistent prior issuances superseded. Effective 15 days after publication, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center. November 9, 2004, Quezon City.

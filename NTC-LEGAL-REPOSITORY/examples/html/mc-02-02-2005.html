Memorandum Circular No. 02-02-2005 — Frequency Allocation and Block Arrangements for Fixed Wireless Access Systems in the Bands 3.4–3.6 GHz and 10.15–10.65 GHz

Status
Active
Issued
February 11, 2005
Legal Basis
RA 7925 and its IRR; EO 546; Act 3846, as amended
Issued By
Commissioner Ronald Olivar Solis; Deputy Commissioners Kathleen G. Heceta, Jorge V. Sarmiento

Rationale

Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) systems in these bands can deliver enhanced telephony and data services. A flexible block (sub-band) arrangement — rather than conventional point-to-point channels — accommodates various FWA technologies while supporting good spectrum management, inter-system operation, and overall efficiency. Standardized block widths simplify inter-system/inter-operator frequency planning.

Allocation and Adoption

Allocates 3.4–3.6 GHz and 10.15–10.65 GHz to FWA systems, forming part of the National Radio Frequency Allocation Table (NRFAT). Adopts block/sub-band edge frequencies divisible by 0.25 MHz per the Channel Plan (Annex I: 3.4–3.6 GHz in 3.5/7/14 MHz block sizes; Annex II: 10.15/10.5 GHz bands in matching block sizes). Frequency blocks are assigned according to system capacity and technology used; where TDD technologies are deployed in the same area, block assignment must account for minimizing interference.

Effectivity

Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 copies to the UP Law Center. Supersedes inconsistent prior issuances. February 11, 2005, Quezon City.

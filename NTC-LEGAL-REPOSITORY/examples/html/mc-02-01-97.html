Memorandum Circular No. 02-01-97 — Licensing Guidelines and Procedures for the Short Range Radio Service (SRRS)

Status
Amended (see MC 1-1-98 and MC 02-01-2014)
Issued
January 14, 1997
Legal Basis
RA 7925, Act 3846, as amended
Issued By
Commissioner Simeon L. Kintanar

Section A — Definitions

Short Range Radio Service (SRRS): a Land Mobile Service radio system for short-range communications. Special Radio Authorization (SRA): authority issued by NTC for possession/operation of an SRRS station.

Section B — Technical Parameters

Frequency use is strictly shared/non-interference basis. Linear amplifiers and external antennas are prohibited. Equipment must have a factory-fixed, non-extendable frequency range, maximum 3-watt power output, portable voice-only operation, and be subject to Commission type approval. Allocated frequency range: 352.0–352.50 MHz on a 12.5 kHz channel plan, with technology-neutral specifications per Attachment A.

Sections C–D — Special Radio Authorization, Importation

SRA is granted through NTC-authorized Radio Communication Equipment Dealers upon purchase. Authorized dealers may import SRRS equipment upon paying import and advanced permit/license fees (₱1,000/unit); locally manufactured units pay the same fee upon stock report submission. Dealers/manufacturers must submit stock inventories and regular sales/stock reports.

Sections E–F — Fees, Administrative Sanctions

One-time advanced permit and license fee: ₱1,000/unit, collected from authorized dealers, who then issue the SRA to the end user. Violations are handled under existing Radio Laws and Regulations.

Effectivity

Effective 15 days after publication; may be revised/revoked/amended in the public interest. January 14, 1997, Quezon City.

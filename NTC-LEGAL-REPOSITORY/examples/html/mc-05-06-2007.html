Memorandum Circular No. 05-06-2007 — Consumer Protection Guidelines

Status
Active
Issued
June 8, 2007
Legal Basis
EO 546, Act 3846, EO 436, RA 7925 and their implementing rules
Issued By
Commissioner Abraham R. Abesamis; Deputy Commissioners Jorge V. Sarmiento, Jaime M. Fortes Jr.

Prescribes consumer welfare and protection rules pursuant to NTC's mandate under EO 546, Act 3846, EO 436, and RA 7925.

Section 1 — Scope and Coverage

Applies to: (1) all Broadcast, CATV, and Public Telecommunications Entities (PTEs) including VAS and Content Providers; (2) all providers, suppliers, mobile service centers, and sellers of telecom/broadcast/CATV equipment including radio dealers and mobile phone suppliers; (3) all consumers, end-users, subscribers, or any person adversely affected by these entities.

Section 2 — Consumer Rights

Establishes: right to equal treatment among similarly situated consumers; data confidentiality and privacy protections with opt-out rights from public directory listings; no charges without express prior agreement (written where practicable); billing strictly per agreed rates/terms; right to select only opted-in services with 30-day penalty-free opt-out from post-paid service; mandatory 30-day advance notice of service changes; free balance-checking for prepaid subscribers; clear and accurate billing for post-paid subscribers; and burden-of-proof rules favoring the subscriber in billing disputes, including a bar on service suspension or adverse credit reporting during pending investigations.

Section 3 — Filing Complaints with the NTC

Consumers must first bring complaints to service providers, who must investigate and act promptly. If unresolved within 30 days, consumers may escalate to the Commission via the One Stop Public Assistance Center (OSPAC). NTC must decide complaints within 15 days of investigation conclusion.

Section 4 — NTC Access to Information

Covered entities must designate a representative to respond to NTC queries on pending complaints. NTC queries require subscriber consent and are limited to information necessary to resolve the complaint; entities must supply relevant records within 10 days of request.

Sections 5–7 — Sanctions, Repeal, Effectivity

Non-compliance is subject to administrative and penal sanctions. This Circular supersedes any inconsistent prior issuances. Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation and filing with the UP Law Center.

Ministry Circular No. 87-174 — Revised Amateur Radio Regulations

Status
Active
Legal Basis
Act No. 3846 as amended by Commonwealth Act No. 571 and EO 546
Issued By
Commissioner Rosauro V. Sibal; Approved by Minister Hernando B. Perez
Repeals
Ministry Circular No. 82-077

Section I — General

Establishes the purpose of the Amateur Radio Service: emergency communications capability, technical skill development, growth of self-trained technologists, and recognition of a single national amateur radio organization for international representation. Sets policy on frequency allocation to licensed amateurs on a non-interference basis, with station licenses granted to NTC-accredited organization members. Defines key terms including amateur operator, amateur fixed/mobile/portable station, repeater station, and trustee.

Section II — Applications and Licenses

No amateur station may operate without a valid NTC permit or license. Defines four operator classes (A–Advanced, B–General, C–Technician, D–Novice), license effectivity of up to 30 years, renewal procedures with tiered fees based on lapse duration, license modification and duplicate procedures, and fee schedules.

Section III — Technical Regulations

Specifies the full amateur frequency band allocation table (1.8 MHz through 250 GHz), authorized power output limits by operator class (Class A: up to 1kW CW / 2kW PEP; Class D: up to 100W), and emergency channel procedures including distress call protocol (MAYDAY) on the 145.000 MHz emergency channel.

Section IV — Amateur Radio Operator Examination

General qualifications: Philippine citizen (or reciprocal foreign national), at least 12 years old, good moral character, and language proficiency. Examination covers 10 elements (Morse Code through Radio Wave Propagation) plus a Morse code speed test (5–12 WPM depending on class) and written exam requiring a 70% average with no element below 50%.

Section V — Rules Governing Operation of Amateur Stations

Requires valid certificate and license for operation; plain-language transmission only; prohibits profane language, false identification, commercial use, and intentional jamming; mandates call sign posting and identification; and sets technical constraints on interference, power output, and frequency stability.

Sections VI–IX — Repeaters, Organizations, Districts, Foreign Amateurs

Covers repeater station authorization (restricted to accredited associations), amateur organization accreditation requirements (SEC registration, 25+ member minimum, Memorandum of Agreement with NTC), the nine amateur radio districts of the Philippines, and reciprocal licensing terms for foreign amateurs temporarily in the Philippines.

Section X — Grounds for Suspension/Revocation of License

Licenses may be revoked for: violations of Act 3846 or related regulations; false statements in applications; unlicensed operation; failure to comply with license conditions; or intentional harmful interference/jamming.

Sections XI–XII — Repealing Clause and Effectivity

This Circular supersedes Ministry Circular No. 82-077 and took effect immediately upon issuance.

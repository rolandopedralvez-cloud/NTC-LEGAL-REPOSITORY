Memorandum Circular No. 19-12-2000 — Revised Schedule of, Including Additional, Administrative Fees and Charges of the National Telecommunications Commission

This is the master administrative fee schedule for nearly all NTC services, issued to comply with the government-wide 20% fee increase directive under EO 197/218. It is referenced as the fee basis by numerous other Circulars in this repository, including MC 04-89, MC 13-09-2004, and successor Circulars for GMDSS training (MC 17-2-2002) and Communications Technician training (MC 16-11-2004).

Section 1 — General Provisions

Defines "power" per service type (transmitter output for AM broadcast; effective radiated power for FM/TV and Fixed/Land Mobile/Maritime/Aeronautical/Space services), with power tiers: High (above 100W), Medium (25–100W), Low (25W and below). Full-duplex narrowband stations are charged double the simplex license fee. Station license modifications trigger the applicable Construction Permit Fee.

Section 2 — Schedule of Fees and Charges

Comprehensive fee tables (Filing Fee, Construction Permit, License Fee, Inspection Fee) covering: Broadcast Services (AM/FM/TV commercial and non-commercial, CATV, TV Translator, Multi-Channel Pay TV/MMDS/LMDS/DTH, TVRO); Fixed Service (public/private, transoceanic/domestic, repeater stations); Land Base & Mobile Services; Maritime Mobile Service (coast stations, ship stations, ship earth stations, various certificates); Aeronautical Mobile/Fixed Services; Radio Navigation/Location Service; Radio Amateur Service (Classes A–D, club stations); Satellite Service (public/private, broadcast fixed satellite); Special Services (radio paging, GMDSS/general radiocommunications training stations, industrial/scientific/medical, remote control devices); Suppliers/Dealers/Manufacturers; Construction Permit applications; Equipment Certification (type approval, laboratory testing); and an extensive list of Various Permits including operator certificates for all classes (GOC, Radiotelegraph, Radiotelephone, RTTY, ROC, RLM, REC, and more), VAS/Paging/Trunk Radio registration, and document authentication fees.

Sections 3–5 — Repealing Clause, Separability, Effectivity

Fees not revised herein remain in effect under existing issuances; conflicting issuances are amended/superseded. Invalid provisions do not affect the remainder. Effective 15 days after publication in a newspaper of general circulation, with 3 certified copies to the UP Law Center.

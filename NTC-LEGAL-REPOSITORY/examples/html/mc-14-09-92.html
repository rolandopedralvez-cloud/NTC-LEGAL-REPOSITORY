Memorandum Circular No. 14-09-92 — Rules and Regulations Governing the Registration and Licensing of Satellite Television Receive Only (TVRO) Stations in the Philippines

Status
Active
Issued
September 14, 1992
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended; EO 546
Issued By
Commissioner Mariano E. Benedicto II

Section 1 — Definition of Terms

Defines a TVRO station as one receiving sound and video signals directly from satellite. Distinguishes Non-Commercial TVRO (personal/family use, government agencies with no fees charged, or foreign government/UN premises-limited use) from Commercial TVRO (operated for monetary gain). Also defines Registration Certificate and TVRO Station License.

Section 2 — General Provisions

Non-commercial TVRO stations require a Registration Certificate; commercial stations require a full TVRO Station License. Only NTC type-approved equipment may be registered. Use of received program material is subject to domestic and international copyright law. Registration fee: ₱5,000 (one-time, both station types). Commercial license: ₱2,000 annual fee, renewable 60 days before expiry.

Section 3 — Accreditation of Dealers, Suppliers, Manufacturers and Service Centers

Dealers, suppliers, manufacturers, and service centers of TVRO equipment must be accredited by the Commission in accordance with MC 02-05-88 and applicable laws.

Section 4 — Procedures for Registration of TVRO Stations

Applicants apply to NTC or any Regional Office using prescribed forms. Grantees must comply with all applicable technical, programming, and other conditions the Commission may impose.

Section 5 — Posting of Registration Certificate

The Registration Certificate or TVRO Station License must be posted conspicuously near the station premises.

Section 6 — Inspection of TVRO Stations

The Commission reserves the right to inspect/investigate TVRO stations during reasonable hours, and may suspend or revoke a registration found in violation of Commission rules.

Section 7 — Sanctions

Stations operating in violation of laws/rules or without valid registration/license shall be closed, suspended, removed, or rendered non-operational. Additional fines apply: up to ₱2,000 for individuals, up to ₱5,000 for companies or corporations, for violations of this Circular or Radio Laws and Regulations (local and international).

Effectivity

The Commission may revise or amend this Circular as the need arises in the interest of public service. This Circular took effect immediately upon approval.

Memorandum Circular No. 12-08-92 — Rules Governing Installation, Operation and Maintenance of a Radio Communication Network by Civic Action Organizations

Status
Active
Issued
August 7, 1992
Legal Basis
Act 3846, as amended
Issued By
Commissioner Mariano E. Benedicto II

Purpose

Governs radiocommunication networks for civic action organizations engaged in public assistance during emergencies/disasters involving life, property, and peace/order preservation.

General Requirements — Authorization

Requires SEC registration with 50+ bona fide active members. Applications need SEC Certificate/Articles/By-laws, member/officer lists, a technical letter of intent with network diagram sealed by an ECE, existing MOAs with government/NGO partners, and NBI clearance for all members (within 3 months of filing).

Permits and Operator Certificates

Requires Permit to Purchase, Construction Permit, Radio Station License, and appropriate Radio Operator's Certificate. Base station operators need at least a Second Class Radio Telephone Operator Certificate; mobile/portable operators need at least a Restricted Land Mobile Certificate. Licenses renew annually with proof of continued MOA and civic activity reports.

Technical Provisions

Allocates specific frequency bands within 400 MHz, 200 MHz, and VHF mid-band ranges, all on a sharing/non-interference basis. Only type-approved equipment permitted (with a grandfathering window for pre-existing amateur equipment through December 11, 1995). VHF mid-band limited to simplex mode. One network per organization; generally one base station per 25 km service radius.

Effectivity

Effective immediately, superseding inconsistent prior issuances. August 7, 1992, Quezon City.

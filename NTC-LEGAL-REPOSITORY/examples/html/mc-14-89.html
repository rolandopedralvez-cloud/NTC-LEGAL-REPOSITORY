Memorandum Circular No. 14-89 — Issuance of Government Radio Operator Certificate (GROC)

Status
Active
Issued
1989
Legal Basis
RA 3846, as amended; Executive Order No. 456
Issued By
Commissioner Jose Luis A. Alcuaz

Sections 1–3 — Purpose and Certificate

No government radio station may operate without a licensed operator holding a valid commercial radiotelephone operator's certificate or Government Radio Operator Certificate (GROC), except military stations. GROC is issued to government employees who pass NTC's special written examination and submit the required application.

Section 4 — Applicant Qualifications

Must be of good moral character and employed in government service as a radio operator for at least 6 months, certified by the Head of Office. Requires an application form and Service Record.

Section 5 — Examination

Special monthly NTC examination covering government radio station regulations and basic technical equipment knowledge.

Section 6 — Validity and Renewal

Issued/renewed for 1 year, up to a maximum of 3 years. Renewal must be filed 30 days before expiry.

Section 7 — SROP Conversion Provisions

Holders of a Special Radio Operator Permit (SROP) acquired before December 31, 1988, who passed Elements 1 and 2, may convert to GROC by submitting the application, original/CTC of SROP, Service Record, and fees — even if not previously issued an SROP, provided they passed the relevant elements before that date.

Effectivity

Supersedes inconsistent prior issuances. Effective immediately upon issuance.

Memorandum Circular No. 11-21-88 — Implementing Rules and Regulations Governing the Authorization, Installation, Operation, etc. of the Personal Radio Service in the Philippines

Status
Active
Issued
July 22, 1988
Issued By
Commissioner Jose Luis A. Alcuaz

Sections 1–2 — Purpose and Definitions

Governs the Personal Radio Service (PRS): short-distance two-way land mobile communications for qualified individuals and small businesses. Defines Personal Base/Land Mobile/Portable Radio Stations, Restricted Land Mobile Permit (RLMP), and Second Class Radiotelephone Operator Certificate.

Section 3 — Applications, Permits/Licenses

Requires Permit to Purchase/Possess, Construction Permit, and Radio Station License before installing/operating a PRS station. Base stations require RLMP-holder operation under a 2nd Class Radiotelephone Operator Certificate holder's supervision; mobile/portable requires at least an RLMP holder. Licenses valid up to 3 years, renewable, at standard commercial station license fees.

Sections 4–5 — Applicant and Technical Requirements

Applicants must be Philippine citizens (18+ if individual, or duly incorporated entities). Technical rules: frequency sharing/non-interference basis, maximum 1 base + 2 mobile + 2 portable stations per system (beyond 5 requires a regular commercial license), authorized power output only (linear amplifiers/boosters strictly prohibited), voice-only emission.

Sections 6–8 — Operating Rules, Sanctions, Effectivity

Plain-language transmission only (English/Pilipino); no profane/indecent content; mandatory call sign identification and posting. Violations subject to fines, cancellation/suspension, and equipment forfeiture; unlicensed operation subject to confiscation and additional sanctions. Effective immediately, Quezon City.

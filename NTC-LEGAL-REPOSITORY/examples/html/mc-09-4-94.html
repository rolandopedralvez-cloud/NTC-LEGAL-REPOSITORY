Memorandum Circular No. 09-4-94 — Additional Guidelines for the Renewal of Amateur License and Application for Amateur Radio Examination

Status
Active
Issued
April 15, 1994
Amends
Guidelines under MC 02-03-87
Issued By
Commissioner Simeon Kintanar

I. Renewal of Licenses

License renewal requires proof of amateur activity: DX (log extracts/QSL cards/awards), Technical Experimentation (documented tests, homebrewing, circuit diagrams), Emergency Communications (certificates, ARES membership), Club Membership (encouraged, optional), or Field Expedition (NTC permit with special DX call sign). Proof must be submitted via Form ARSCP-001, endorsed by a recognized Amateur Radio Club or Class A license holder, who bears responsibility for the endorsement.

II. Orientation Seminar for Applicants

Class C/D examination applicants must attend an orientation seminar conducted by a recognized club or Class A holder with proper endorsement, per a standardized curriculum set by the Amateur Radio Consultative Panel and approved by the Commission. See related course structure in MC 87-174.

III. Amateur Radio Consultative Panel (ARCP)

Five-member panel: 2 Commission representatives, 2 Class A amateurs recommended by PARA, and 1 outstanding Class A amateur jointly recommended by all four. ARCP recommends which clubs/individuals may endorse license renewals and conduct seminars; authorization can be revoked for MOA non-compliance, abuse of endorsement privileges, or endorsements issued for financial gain. Misrepresentation in endorsements is grounds for revocation/suspension of the endorsing party's accreditation or license.

Effectivity

Effective immediately upon approval, superseding inconsistent prior issuances. April 15, 1994, Quezon City. Includes Form ARSCP-001 (Proof of Amateur Activity) as an attachment.
